In an early run of a centralized-critic MAPPO policy for three-robot area coverage in Gazebo, policy entropy fell to -0.53 and we attributed this to RLlib's default GAE lambda (1.0) and entropy coefficient (0.0). The raw logs were not kept and the attribution was never tested against a control. We test it with six training runs, three per configuration (a reconstruction of the original configuration and a five-change "corrected" configuration), logging per-iteration metrics. We did not observe the historical low-entropy pattern: entropy at iteration 15 was 1.50-1.87 under the reconstruction and 1.95-2.48 under the correction. With three runs per arm the smallest attainable permutation p-value is 0.10, the corrected arm received 2.4x as many environment steps, five hyperparameters changed at once, and no task metric was logged, so no mechanism or benefit can be attributed. The original diagnosis is not supported; run-to-run entropy variability is large enough for a single run to mislead. A companion to the author's negative-result coverage study.
